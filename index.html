Research that partners put to work

I am an industrial engineer and researcher working at the intersection of optimization, decision science, data analytics, and artificial intelligence. My experience spans industry, research, and engineering education in Türkiye and the United States. I currently serve as an Instructor at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, following academic appointments at Erciyes University and a research fellowship at Auburn University. My work connects analytical methods with customer retention, disaster resilience, and engineering decisions.

Operations Research & Decision Science

Mathematical optimization, multi-criteria decision analysis, uncertainty, and resource allocation connect my work on logistics center location, customer-informed product design, shelter allocation, and last-mile relief distribution.

          Engineering Economy

          A semester-long "SmartCampus Café" investment project where student teams make AI-assisted decisions under uncertainty. A "Verified by Human" protocol, interaction logs, and a three-level engagement rubric train students to check and correct AI outputs instead of accepting them.

          AI Stakeholder Simulation

          Project Management Planning & Control

          Generative-AI role-play in which students negotiate with simulated stakeholders, such as frustrated clients and resistant team members, to build conflict resolution, ethical judgment, and leadership. Includes a shareable prompt library for college-wide use.
